## Ideas for level manager

Currently there is one level per run, and the run ends at the end of the level (success or failure)

That hard cap seems anti-fun

I suggest making it an endless mode, so at end of succesful level you get to go to next level

All enemies have 10% more health and 10% more damage, and player has 10% more speed. Player carries over gold. 

Also you get to pick which weapon you would like to carry over

I suggest putting that logic inside of a LevelManager

Obviously UI needs to show the current level
